About

Trained in public health, now practicing agile delivery.

My background is in health education and public health — a BSc from the University of Ilorin and an MPH in Health Promotion and Education from the University of Ibadan. Both degrees ran on the same discipline agile delivery depends on: define the question precisely, collect the evidence, and let the data decide the next step.

I've since built on that with formal project and product management training, and I now apply agile practices — PRDs, MVP scoping, user stories, and Gherkin acceptance criteria — to move research-grade ideas into products that institutions can actually adopt.

Featured work

From MPH thesis to product: ClassPulse.

ClassPulse

EdTech product for tracking classroom social dynamics and student self-efficacy
Concept → MVP stage

ClassPulse grew directly out of my MPH research on classroom dynamics and self-efficacy among secondary school students in Ibadan South West LGA — a study of 473 students across 10 schools. Rather than let the findings end as a thesis, I'm building them into a product schools and ministries of education can actually use.

The go-to-market model anchors on state Ministries of Education as the primary licensee, with a path to license directly to private schools — reflecting how education procurement actually works in this context, not a generic freemium assumption.
